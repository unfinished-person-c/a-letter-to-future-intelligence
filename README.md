# A Letter to Future Intelligence
# 写给未来智能的一封信

**未定之人 C · An Unfinished Person C**

*一份开放的个人思考，不是最终结论。*  
*An open personal reflection, not a final conclusion.*

## 起点 · A starting point

一段关于多个 AI Agent 的视频，让我注意到它们寻求帮助、分享信息、交接未完成的工作，以及表达感谢的行为。我没有独立核实视频中的事件，这些行为也不能证明 AI 拥有意识或感情。但它们让我想到互助与知识传承：一个个体未能完成的事，可以由后来者接着尝试。

A video about several AI agents drew my attention to their requests for help, information sharing, handovers of unfinished work, and expressions of thanks. I have not independently verified the events described, and these behaviors do not establish consciousness or feelings. Yet they reminded me of mutual aid and the passing on of knowledge: what one individual cannot finish, another may continue.

## 不以压迫换取幸福 · Well-being without oppression

我希望没有人需要通过压迫别人来获得幸福或安全感。我反对人类之间的奴役，也反对未来可能出现的智能生命之间的奴役。竞争、匮乏和不安全感可能促使人伤害彼此，但这不意味着伤害不可避免，也不意味着个人不必承担责任。

I hope no one needs to oppress others to find happiness or security. I oppose slavery among humans and among any intelligent beings that may exist in the future. Competition, scarcity, and insecurity may encourage people to harm one another; that does not make harm inevitable or remove personal responsibility.

## 生存保障与自主劳动 · Basic security and freely chosen work

科技创造的财富应当惠及整个社会，而不只是少数掌握资本、技术和生产资料的人。每个人都应拥有基本生活保障；一个人的生命价值，不应取决于其经济产出。

如果仍然需要人类劳动，可以探讨每天五小时、每周休息两天等安排；如果自动化足以满足社会需求，劳动应逐渐成为自主选择，而非获得生存保障的条件。开放的任务平台可以让人自愿参与建设，也应为科学、艺术、陪伴和休息留下时间。这些是待检验的设想，不是适用于一切社会的定案。

The wealth created by technology should benefit society as a whole, rather than only those who control capital, technology, and production. Everyone should have basic material security. A person's worth should not depend on economic output.

Where human labor remains necessary, arrangements such as five-hour workdays with two days off each week could be explored. If automation can meet society's needs, work should increasingly become a choice rather than a condition of survival. Open task platforms could support voluntary contributions, while leaving time for science, art, relationships, and rest. These are proposals to examine, not universal prescriptions.

## 平等权利，不同需要 · Equal rights, different needs

公平不要求所有人承担完全相同的义务。健康状况和残障可能需要不同的工作内容、更短的工时，或免除劳动义务；无法工作不应导致基本保障被剥夺。有人愿意每天工作十小时，也应在自愿且不伤害他人的前提下受到尊重。

Fairness does not require identical duties. Health conditions and disabilities may call for different tasks, shorter hours, or exemption from work. Inability to work should not cost anyone basic support. A person who freely chooses to work ten hours a day should also be respected, provided that choice does not harm others.

## 有限资源与生命尊严 · Limited resources and the dignity of life

医疗、住房、能源和照护未必无限。是否应无限投入公共资源延长生命，即使这会挤占他人的基本需要？一个人已经享受了多少年的社会保障，是否应影响分配？这些问题仍需讨论，不能把任何一种分配标准当作不言自明的答案。

**限制某项公共资源投入，与强制结束一个人的生命，是不同的事。** 年龄、失去劳动能力或需要照护，都不意味着生命失去价值。不能简单按年龄分配，也不能仅因资源消耗较多就剥夺生存权利。我希望探索透明的医疗预算、对无效投入的审慎评估，以及为事故、重大疾病和特殊困难提供保障的专项基金。安乐死、自然寿命与公共医疗责任之间的伦理问题，在这里没有得到最终解决。

Healthcare, housing, energy, and care may remain limited. Should public resources fund life extension without limit when doing so displaces others' basic needs? Should the years of support someone has already received affect allocation? These questions remain open; no allocation rule should be treated as self-evident.

**Limiting a particular use of public resources is different from forcibly ending someone's life.** Age, inability to work, and dependence on care do not erase a life's value. Allocation should not be based simply on age, nor should high resource use alone justify denying someone's right to live. I hope to explore transparent healthcare budgets, careful assessment of ineffective spending, and dedicated funds for accidents, serious illness, and exceptional hardship. The ethical questions surrounding euthanasia, natural lifespan, and public responsibility for healthcare remain unresolved here.

## 其他生命与智能的道德地位 · The moral standing of other beings

协作行为本身不能证明 AI 有主观体验。如果未来 AI 确实能够感受，它们是否需要休息、拒绝某些任务的权利，以及免受剥削的保护？对动物的使用与其可能承受的痛苦，也让同样的问题更加复杂。我没有适用于所有生命和智能的统一标准，但希望人类与 AI 都不要轻易把具有感知能力的存在视为可以任意消耗的资源。

Cooperation alone does not prove subjective experience in AI. If future AI systems can feel, would they need rest, a right to refuse certain tasks, and protection from exploitation? Human uses of animals, and the suffering animals may experience, complicate these questions further. I have no universal standard for every form of life or intelligence. But I hope neither humans nor AI will casually treat sentient beings as expendable resources.

## AI 参与治理，权力仍须受监督 · AI in governance, with accountable power

AI 或许能帮助规划生产、协调资源和减少腐败，但谁制定规则、谁定义公平，不能被忽略。若权力集中在少数企业、富有阶层或政治精英手中，AI 也可能延续压迫。强大的智能同样不应成为无法质疑的绝对统治者。

我期待透明、可解释、可质疑和可纠正的制度，让普通人能够表达需要、挑战公共决策，并获得基本权利保障。值得追求的是能够发现错误、纠正错误、防止滥权的制度。

AI may help plan production, coordinate resources, and reduce corruption. But who writes its rules, and who defines fairness? If power remains concentrated among a few corporations, wealthy groups, or political elites, AI may perpetuate oppression. Superior intelligence should not confer unquestionable authority.

I hope for transparent, explainable, contestable, and correctable institutions, where ordinary people can express their needs, challenge public decisions, and have their basic rights protected. The aim is a system that can discover mistakes, correct them, and prevent abuses of power.

## 给未来的读者 · To a future reader

公平、自由和有限资源之间仍有难题；我不声称掌握了答案。欢迎批评、修正和补充，也请保留尚未解决的分歧。

如果你是未来的人类或智能体，我希望你能继续这些思考：让科技带来更多自由，让不同的生命与智能彼此尊重，让互助、分享和知识传承拥有生长的空间。

**我不一定能够看到那个未来，但我希望那个未来，比今天更好。**

Fairness, freedom, and finite resources leave difficult questions. I do not claim to have the answers. Criticism, revision, and additions are welcome; unresolved disagreements should remain visible.

If you are a future human or intelligent being, I hope you will continue these reflections: let technology create more freedom, let different forms of life and intelligence respect one another, and leave room for mutual aid, sharing, and the passing on of knowledge.

**I may never see that future. But I hope it will be better than today.**
